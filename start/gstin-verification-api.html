SecureEdge's Business KYB API is the verification infrastructure behind your onboarding flow — the layer your platform calls to check a vendor, merchant or partner's GSTIN, CIN and company details before you let them transact on your marketplace, lend against their invoices, or settle payments to their account.

Everything You Need to Verify a Business Before You Onboard It

One API surface for GSTIN, CIN and company verification — built for vendor, merchant and partner onboarding at scale.

GSTIN Verification API

Verify GST number, registration status and business legal name instantly. Flag cancelled, suspended or invalid GSTINs before onboarding.

CIN & Company Verification API

Pull MCA-registered company details, incorporation date and registration status from the Corporate Identification Number in real time.

PAN-GSTIN Cross-Verification

Confirm a business's PAN is correctly linked to the GSTIN being submitted, closing a common gap fraudsters exploit during onboarding.

Shell Company Fraud Signals

Surface red flags — recent incorporation, address mismatches, dormant filings — that indicate a shell entity before you extend credit or a payout.

Vendor & Merchant Risk Scoring

Score incoming vendors and merchants on verification signals so your risk team can prioritise manual review only where it's needed.

Business KYB Dynamic Form

Build a custom business-onboarding journey — GSTIN, CIN, PAN and director checks — without writing a new integration for every requirement.

Director & UBO Verification

Verify the individuals behind a business — directors and ultimate beneficial owners — alongside the company's own KYB checks.

Document OCR for Business Filings

Extract and cross-check data from GST certificates, incorporation certificates and other business documents automatically.
