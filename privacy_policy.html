Privacy Policy

Your Data. Our Responsibility.

At Secureedge Fintech, privacy is not just a compliance requirement — it is a commitment. As a fintech company working with digital and financial ecosystems, we understand the importance of safeguarding information with integrity, transparency, and accountability. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your information when you visit our website, interact with our team, or use our services.

SECUREDGE

Introduction

By accessing or using our website and services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, we recommend discontinuing the use of our website and services. This policy applies to all visitors, clients, partners, and users who interact with our digital platforms.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out forms, request a consultation, communicate with us, or engage with our services. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company details, professional information, and any other details you choose to share.

In addition, certain technical information is collected automatically when you access our website. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and browsing behavior such as pages visited and time spent. This data helps us improve functionality, security, and overall user experience.

Where required for service delivery or regulatory compliance, we may also process transaction-related or financial information.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to deliver and enhance our fintech solutions, respond to inquiries, process service requests, and provide customer support. We also use data to improve platform performance, ensure system security, detect fraud, comply with legal obligations, and communicate important service-related updates. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to optimize performance and understand user engagement. Cookies help us recognize returning visitors, analyze traffic trends, and enhance usability. You may modify your browser settings to decline cookies; however, certain features of the website may not function properly without them.

Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information. These include encrypted communication channels, secure servers, restricted access controls, and regular monitoring of systems to prevent unauthorized access.

While we strive to protect your data, no digital transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We continuously review and strengthen our security framework to minimize potential risks.

Sharing of Information

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, delivering services, processing payments, or ensuring compliance. These partners are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, regulatory authorities, or legal processes. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring, data may be transferred as part of business continuity arrangements.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal and regulatory requirements, and support legitimate business operations. When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain communications. Requests can be made through the contact details provided below.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or our services. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
