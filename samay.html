Why Samay Rail

SE Samay Rail – Digital Railway Operations Platform

SE Samay Rail is purpose-built for Indian Railway Station Masters. Every register that used to live in a bound ledger on the SM's desk — the Train Signal Register (TSR), the private-number booklet, the block demand slips, the caution order log, the key register — is now a live digital form that syncs instantly with the neighbouring station, the control room and the concerned engineer. The platform eliminates illegible handwriting, missing signatures and slow paper handovers. Every action is timestamped, signed, and auditable. Mistakes are caught at data-entry time through strict validation rules taken directly from railway operating procedure.

How it’s work

Seamless Coordination of Train Movements Across Stations

SE Samay enables a seamless, real-time workflow for recording train movements across stations. From the moment a train is proposed by the rear station to its final arrival at the advance station, every step is digitally captured and synchronized between both Station Masters. Line clear exchanges, private numbers, train start, section entry, and arrival are all updated instantly on a shared record, ensuring complete visibility and coordination. Each action is time-stamped and validated, eliminating manual errors and delays. Once the train arrives, the entry is automatically completed and securely locked, with the full Train Signal Register available for export at any time.

Rear SM proposes train → Advance SM acknowledges → Train starts & enters section → Train arrives → TSR complete

Core Module

Train Signal Register (TSR)

The TSR is the heart of the Station Master's day. SE Samay's TSR is a live grid that shows every train in UP and DOWN direction, with each row covering the full life of one train through the section.

Advance form — to the next station
  • Issue line clear with auto-suggested next station
  • Capture advance private number (numeric, validated)
  • Capture advance token number at manual stations
  • Auto-block submission if a previous train is still in the section
  • Auto-skip LC gate prompt at notified stations (CKRD, BPGJ)
  • Log train-started and entering-section-sent times
Rear form — from the previous station
  • Receive rear line clear and acknowledge private number
  • Log entering-section-received and out-of-section-sent
  • Record train arrival on a specific line / platform
  • Auto-sync with the rear station's advance row
  • Cancel train, terminate or mark originating when required
  • Add a remarks row for unusual occurrences
