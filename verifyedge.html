Where Verify Edge Fits Into Your Workflow

Identity verification isn't a single step — it shows up at different points across lending, onboarding and agent-network operations. Here's how banks, NBFCs and fintechs typically use Verify Edge's Aadhaar, PAN, driving license, address, employment and document checks.

NBFC & Digital Lending Onboarding

Loan applications run Aadhaar, PAN and address verification against government and trusted databases during underwriting, so credit teams can move an applicant from application to approval without a manual document-checking queue.

Digital Account Opening Checks

Where a bank or NBFC's account-opening flow needs an identity check as one step in the process, Verify Edge supplies that verification layer — Aadhaar and PAN validation, plus document checks — that a broader onboarding platform can call as needed.

BC Agent & Retail Network Onboarding

Before a business correspondent agent or retail touchpoint goes live on an AEPS network, police verification and document validation help confirm the operator is who they say they are — relevant given RBI's tightened due-diligence expectations for AePS touchpoint operators.

Employment & Platform Verification

Employers, NBFCs assessing salaried borrowers, and gig or platform businesses use employment verification to confirm a person's stated employment before extending credit or onboarding them as a partner.
