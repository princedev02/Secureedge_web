DPDP Management System

Built to Make Data Consent More Transparent

Our platform simplifies how individuals give, manage, review, and withdraw consent for the processing of their personal data. Users can understand what data is being requested, why it is needed, and who is requesting it.

It also maintains records of consent decisions, consent notices, and data sharing, while helping users understand how long their personal data may be retained. Users can easily review their consent history, track their choices, and stay informed about how their data is being handled.

This gives individuals greater visibility and control over their personal data, while helping organizations manage consent-related processes in a clear and structured way.

FAQs

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

1.What is consent management?

Consent management helps users understand, give, manage, and withdraw permission for how their personal data is collected and used.

2.How does DPDP apply to users?

The DPDP framework concerns the processing of personal data. Our platform helps individuals review data requests, understand their purpose, and manage their consent choices.

3.Can users withdraw consent?

Users can review and withdraw their consent through the platform. The organization can then manage the request according to applicable processing and retention requirements.

4.How long is data retained?

Retention depends on the purpose of processing and applicable legal or regulatory requirements. The platform helps make the relevant retention information visible to users.

5.What data can users request?

Users can review the information associated with their consent requests, including the stated purpose, requesting organization, consent status and available history.
