AEPS Solution Provider

Why choose BC Edge?

BC Edge empowers banks and BCs to deliver effortless, real-time banking at customer touchpoints. As a trusted AEPS Solution Provider, we enable balance checks, mini statements, ATM PIN changes, Green PIN generation, chequebook requests, cheque stopping, and secure password updates— making every service simple and accessible. By merging advanced fintech capability with on-ground convenience, BC Edge strengthens financial inclusion, boosts operational efficiency, and creates a seamless digital banking experience across both urban and rural communities. If your bank or BC network is weighing AEPS against a Micro-ATM rollout, BC Edge is the platform that sits behind either setup.
